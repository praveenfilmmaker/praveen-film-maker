🎬The Beginning

சகாப்தம் (Sagaaptham) marks the beginning of my journey in cinema.

My primary passion is Direction and Storytelling. I am interested in creating stories, developing characters and bringing my vision to the screen.

Alongside direction, I also have a strong interest in Acting and enjoy exploring characters and performance as part of my filmmaking journey.

This film is one of my first steps towards learning, creating and growing in cinema.

This is just the beginning of my journey. 🎬

About

A short film about family, influence, mistakes, guilt and realizing the value of loved ones.

My Role

- Direction
- Story & Screenplay
- Editing,Acting

Project

This short film is part of my filmmaking journey and my exploration of storytelling through short films.
